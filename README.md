# CommitGraph

### Persistent Meeting Commitment Memory Agent

CommitGraph is an AI-powered meeting assistant that remembers commitments across meetings, tracks their status, and prepares project managers for future conversations.

Instead of treating every meeting as a separate interaction, CommitGraph builds persistent memory around people, commitments, deadlines, dependencies, and follow-ups.

---

## The Problem

Important commitments are often buried inside meeting notes.

A project manager may remember that someone promised to complete a task, but after several meetings it becomes difficult to remember:

- What exactly was promised?
- Who made the commitment?
- What was the deadline?
- Was it completed?
- Is it still unresolved?
- What should be discussed in the next meeting?

Traditional meeting notes store information, but they do not actively connect past commitments with future conversations.
